Controls

Camera

WASD or screen edge to pan · QE rotate · wheel zoom · middle-drag orbit

Select

Click or drag a box · double-click selects the type · Ctrl+1–9 sets a group, 1–9 recalls it · Tab folds the roster

Move

Right-click to move or attack · right-drag a formation line · R run · vehicles back up on their own for short moves back or out of contact · X halt · Space pause · enemies not yet identified carry a ?: right-click one for area fire

Orders

F hold fire · G garrison · M mount · B resupply · V smoke · C fire at ground · T mortar · Y artillery (152 / 155 mm)

Deploy

Place units inside your staging ground, then start the battle with Enter

Support

Kornet / Stugna teams set up when halted and engage armour to ~1.2 km. FPV teams launch ten drones; right-click a target to pick one · N show / hide the drone view, Shift+N next drone in the air, click the feed to go to the drone
